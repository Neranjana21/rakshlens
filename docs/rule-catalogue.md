# Rule catalogue

_Generated by `python scripts/gen_rule_catalogue.py` from the code. Do not edit by hand._

Weights are hand-set heuristics that add up to a 0-100 score. They are **not** calibrated probabilities. Bands: Low < 20, Needs verification 20-44, High 45-69, Critical >= 70. A rule with `forces` sets a minimum band on its own. `quote-prone` rules are down-weighted (and quoted phrases removed) when the message is clearly a warning or awareness post.

| Rule | Group | Weight | Severity | Forces band | Quote-prone | What the user is told |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `GUARANTEED_RETURNS` | returns | 30 | strong | - | yes | **Promises guaranteed or risk-free profit.** Markets can lose money, and no one can honestly promise a fixed profit. SEBI itself warns that guaranteed-return claims are a red flag. |
| `UNREALISTIC_RETURN_RATE` | returns | 25 | strong | - | yes | **Promises very high returns in a short time.** These returns are many times what ordinary investments earn. Promises like this are used to lure people in. |
| `PROFIT_PROOF` | returns | 10 | warning | - | yes | **Shows 'profit proof' to win your trust.** Screenshots and testimonials are easy to fake and prove nothing. |
| `SEBI_CLAIM_NO_NUMBER` | authority | 15 | unverified | verify | - | **Says it is SEBI-approved or registered, but gives no registration number.** Anyone can write 'SEBI approved'. We could not check this claim. Ask for the registration number and search it on sebi.gov.in. |
| `SEBI_NUMBER_UNVERIFIED` | authority | 5 | unverified | verify | - | **Gives a registration number we could not verify.** The number looks like a SEBI number, but we only check its format, and a fake can look the same. Search it on sebi.gov.in and check that the name and contact details match. |
| `SEBI_NUMBER_BAD_FORMAT` | authority | 25 | strong | verify | - | **Gives a registration number in the wrong format.** SEBI numbers start with IN, followed by a letter and 9 digits. This one does not fit, so it may be invented or mistyped. |
| `INSIDER_OR_OPERATOR_CLAIM` | authority | 25 | strong | - | - | **Claims inside information or 'operator' backing.** Trading on inside information is illegal, and an honest adviser would not offer it. This is used to make an ordinary tip sound special. |

| `WITHDRAWAL_BLOCKED_FEE` | payment | 35 | strong | high | - | **Asks for a fee or tax to release your money.** Genuine platforms take charges from your balance. Demanding more money so you can withdraw is a known scam. |

| `PERSONAL_PAYEE` | payment | 20 | warning | - | - | **Asks you to pay into a UPI ID or bank account given in chat.** Check the name that appears before you pay. Paying a stranger for 'access' or 'tips' is very risky. |

| `REFERRAL_SCHEME` | pressure | 25 | strong | - | yes | **Rewards you for bringing in other people.** Schemes that pay a bonus for adding friends or members are how Ponzi and pyramid schemes grow. The money paid to early members comes from the people who join later. |
